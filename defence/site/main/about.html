About us

Thirty years, one office, a great deal of paperwork.

Siwang Trading Co. was founded in 1994 by Choo Siew Wang, who spent the previous fifteen years as a shipping clerk and had strong opinions about how the job should be done. The company is still family-held. We employ thirty-one people, all of them in Singapore, and we have never operated a branch office — when something goes wrong on a shipment, the person who booked it is the person who fixes it.

How we work

We are deliberately small for the volume we handle. Every customer has a named account handler who owns their jobs end to end, rather than a queue and a ticket number. That model only works if our internal records are immaculate, which is why we have spent rather more on document management than a company our size normally would.

The document project

The 2011 fire is the reason we care about this more than most. We hold roughly 1.4 million scanned pages against 60,000 job references, and until recently finding any one of them meant asking a person who knew where it was. The replacement system is being built for us by an external development partner and is in staged rollout — internal users first, customer-facing retrieval later.

If you are an existing customer and need a document from before 2024, contact your account handler. They will still know where it is.
