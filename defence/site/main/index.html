Ocean freight

FCL and LCL consolidation out of PSA and Jurong Port, with weekly departures on the intra-Asia lanes and fortnightly consolidations to North Europe. We book space, we chase the carrier, and we tell you before the ETA slips rather than after.

Air freight

Changi-based airside handling for time-critical and temperature- controlled consignments, including dangerous goods under IATA DGR. Same-day airway bill issue on bookings confirmed before 15:00 SGT.

Customs & compliance

Licensed declaring agent under Singapore Customs. Permit applications, classification advice, preferential origin under the region's FTAs, and the kind of tariff-code argument nobody wants to have twice.

Documents, tracked properly

Every consignment we handle carries a document set — bills of lading, packing lists, certificates of origin, permits, delivery notes. Losing one of them costs more than losing the container.

We are part-way through moving thirty years of that paperwork into a single document-control system, so customers can pull any document against any job reference without emailing a person to go and look. Rollout continues through 2025.
