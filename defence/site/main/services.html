Services

Door to door, or any two points in between.

Ocean freight

Full-container and consolidated shipments through PSA terminals and Jurong Port. We hold weekly allocation on the intra-Asia lanes and fortnightly consolidation slots to North Europe and the US west coast. Reefer, flat-rack and open-top equipment available on request.

Air freight

Changi-based handling for consignments that cannot wait for a vessel. Dangerous goods accepted under IATA DGR by our certified staff. Airway bills are issued same-day for bookings confirmed before 15:00 SGT, and we hold standing arrangements with three carriers for AOG cargo.

Customs brokerage

Siwang is a licensed declaring agent with Singapore Customs. We handle permit applications through TradeNet, tariff classification, valuation rulings, and preferential origin claims under the region's free trade agreements. Where a classification is genuinely arguable we will tell you that up front, along with what it costs to be wrong.

Bonded warehousing

Two licensed facilities — Tuas and Keppel Distripark — offering duty suspension, pick-and-pack, container stuffing and unstuffing, and fumigation to ISPM 15 where required.

Project and out-of-gauge cargo

Route surveys, lift planning and permit coordination for cargo that will not fit in a box. Recent work includes transformer sets to Sarawak and brewery vessels into Vietnam.

Documentation

Every job carries a document set, and every document is retained for the statutory five years. We are consolidating that archive into a single document-control system so customers can retrieve any document against a job reference directly. Rollout continues through 2025; until it is complete, please continue to request documents through your account handler.
